Coverage is a monthly-phased read: monthly target = 2H26 goal ÷ 6; gap = monthly target − September Closed Won; coverage = open September pipeline ÷ gap (a seat already at target shows no gap and is "At/over goal"). Website coverage = open website opportunities ÷ (VP target − sold). Figures aggregate by Client Partner and roll to region per the hierarchy; off-roster owners are folded into their region.
