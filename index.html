        Method. Coverage is a monthly-phased read: monthly target = 2H26 goal ÷ 6; gap = monthly target − October Closed Won; coverage = open October pipeline ÷ gap (a seat already at target shows no gap and is "At/over goal"). Expected (weighted) coverage discounts open pipeline by stage — Closing 80%, Presentation 40%, Follow-Up 25%, Discovery 10% — to reflect realistic close probability this month. Website coverage = open website opportunities ÷ (VP target − sold). Figures aggregate by Client Partner and roll to region per the Hierarchy tab. Only individuals listed in the Hierarchy tab are included — pipeline, bookings and website counts owned by anyone else (e.g. managers or former seat holders) are excluded, and vacant seats appear as Open. Source: CPPipelineTemplate1052026v3.xlsx, all October close dates; October Closed Won comes from the Actuals tab. Included pipeline reconciles to $2,383,260 (open $2,366,208 + Closed Won $17,052 in the pipeline pull).
