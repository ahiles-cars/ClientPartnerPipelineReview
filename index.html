Method. Coverage = open October website opportunities ÷ (VP regional target − websites sold); a region at or above target reads "At/over goal". Websites are opportunities with Product Offering = Website, counted once per opportunity (not per quote line). Sold = unique website opportunities closed in October per the Actuals tab, combined with Closed Won website opportunities in the pipeline pull (no double counting). Open = every other stage. Expected (weighted) coverage discounts open opportunities by stage — Closing 80%, Presentation 40%, Follow-Up 25%, Discovery 10% — shown in the hover detail. Regions roll up per the Hierarchy tab, and only individuals listed there are counted; website opportunities owned by anyone else are excluded. Targets come from the Target tab (October Website Target by Region). Source: CPPipelineTemplate1072026.xlsx, Pipeline1072026 tab (all October close dates).
